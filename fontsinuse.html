My Fonts in Use

I’ve made a few fonts and on occasion I see them being used in the wild. This is always a pleasant surprise, and truly one of the greatest thrills graphic design has to offer. I’ve been keeping track of favorite and notable uses and it seemed like a good thing to share on my own website. So check it out.

Note: I was not involved in the actual design of any of this stuff, I just made the font that was used. Shoutout to all the designers involved. Credit and links included below.

Monsters of Drakkenheim
by Dungeon Dudes

Geizer has been heavily used throughout the Dungeons of Drakkenheim TTRPG series since 2021. It's super exciting to see a font I made used so frequently and expertly. I’m most excited about its use in the printed books, but they basically made a lyric video for their latest release, Monsters of Drakkenheim. And that seemed to be the most appropriate thing to showcase here.

Did it First
by Ice Spice

Hypik was used (very briefly) in the music and lyric video for Did It First by Ice Spice and Central Cee. At the time of writing, the music video has 69 million views. Nice!

Dungeon Master Mug
by Elbenwald

Alyson and I visited Austria in 2025 and were walking around a mall in Vienna when I stumbled across this mug in person. Extremely neat to be so far from home and come into contact with a little piece of your work and be able to actually interact with it physically. There have been other exciting discoveries, most of which are listed on this page, but the fact that this one happened in real life will always be special.

HYPERDRIVE
by snerds

Hyperdrive is an extremely cool indie shooter developed by snerds. The title screen uses an 8-bit version of Hypik and it’s a perfect execution. The colors, music, and overall aesthetic are on point. Great work, snerds! Thanks for using Hypik in such a cool way. Maybe some day I'll make a full 8-bit version of Hypik, inspired by this.

Curse of Dreadbear
by Steel Wool Studios

Geizer was used on the cover art and logo for the Curse of Dreadbear, a DLC for Five Nights at Freddy's: Help Wanted. I’m not a FNAF guy, but I will always be thrilled to see anything I’ve made show up in a video game of any kind. Check out the developer's website here.

Supernatural
by NewJeans (뉴진스)

Hypik was used in the Supernatural music video by South Korean girl group NewJeans. And at over 105 million views, this is almost certainly the biggest splash Hypik has ever made. However, it is worth noting that the type has been heavily modified and it barely looks like Hypik anymore. I probably wouldn’t have caught this one, if not for this tweet identifying the font.
